About Gamerock Welfare Association

Building stronger communities through welfare, unity, and sustainable development since our founding.

Mission Vision Values Impact Privacy Terms

Our Mission

To empower communities through sustainable development programs, financial support, and social welfare initiatives that uplift every member of our community. We are committed to creating lasting positive change by fostering collaboration, transparency, and collective responsibility among all members.

Our Vision

To build stronger, more resilient communities where every member has access to the resources and support they need to thrive. We envision a future where no one is left behind, and where community bonds grow stronger with each passing day through shared purpose and mutual support.

Our Values

Transparency

Accountability

Responsible stewards of community resources and trust.

Growth

Committed to collective development and continuous improvement.

Community First

Every decision we make prioritizes the well-being of our members.

What We Do

We run practical programs that touch every stage of community life — from onboarding new members to sustaining long-term welfare support.

Member Registration

Contribution Tracking

Community Events

Regular gatherings, fundraisers, and welfare drives that bring members together.

Welfare Support

Targeted assistance for members and families through structured welfare programs.

Our Impact

Since our establishment, Gamerock Welfare Association has grown into a vibrant community of members united by a common purpose. Through collective contributions, community events, and welfare programs, we have made a meaningful difference in the lives of many families.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the member information required to run the welfare association: names, national ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, family details and contribution records. This information is used to administer membership, record contributions and organise community events.

Member data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Sensitive identifiers such as ID and phone numbers are masked for non-administrator accounts, and only administrators may view or edit full records. If you would like a copy of your data or want it corrected, please contact us and we will respond promptly.

Terms of Service

Membership of Gamerock Welfare Association is voluntary and requires accurate registration details, timely contributions and respectful participation in community activities. Contributions recorded on this platform are receipted against a specific community event and are non-refundable unless the association resolves otherwise.

Your account is personal to you. Keep your password confidential and notify an administrator immediately if you suspect unauthorised access. Accounts used to submit false records or to misuse member information may be suspended. Continued use of this platform confirms your acceptance of these terms.
